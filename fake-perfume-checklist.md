# Fake-perfume checklist

A practical buyer checklist before you pay. Pair this with the full guide: [How to spot fake perfume](https://uaeperfume.ae/blogs/guides/how-to-spot-fake-perfume) and [Where to buy original perfume in the UAE](https://uaeperfume.ae/blogs/guides/where-to-buy-original-perfume-uae).

## Before you buy

- [ ] Seller has a clear return or authenticity policy.
- [ ] Price is not “too good to be true” versus known market range for that bottle.
- [ ] Packaging photos show batch/lot codes, seals, and crisp printing (no blurry logos).
- [ ] Prefer established retailers or brand boutiques over anonymous social DMs.
- [ ] For online orders, confirm the domain and payment page look legitimate.

## When the bottle arrives

- [ ] Cellophane / seal looks factory-applied (not loose or re-taped).
- [ ] Cap, sprayer, and glass weight feel consistent with other authentic bottles you own.
- [ ] Label alignment and typography match brand references (spelling, spacing, fonts).
- [ ] Batch code on box and bottle match (when both are present).
- [ ] Juice color is clear (unless the brand’s formula is intentionally tinted) — cloudy or separated juice is a red flag.

## On skin

- [ ] First spray smells like alcohol blast only, then nothing / harsh chemical — suspicious.
- [ ] Longevity collapses in under an hour on clean skin — possible dilution (not proof alone).
- [ ] Compare to a known authentic sample if you can access one.

## Red flags (walk away)

- No receipt or sealed packaging for a “new” luxury bottle.
- Seller refuses returns “because fragrance.”
- Pressure to pay only via irreversible methods with no buyer protection.
