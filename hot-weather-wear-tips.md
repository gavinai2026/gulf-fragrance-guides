# Hot-weather wear tips (Gulf climate)

Practical tips for perfume in heat and humidity. Full article: [Best perfume for hot weather](https://uaeperfume.ae/blogs/guides/best-perfume-for-hot-weather).

## Quick rules

1. **Less is more.** Heat amplifies projection; one or two sprays often beat four.
2. **Fresher tops, lighter bases.** Citrus, green, tea, light florals, and airy musks usually wear cleaner than heavy ambers at midday.
3. **Clothes over skin for longevity.** A light mist on fabric (test colorfastness first) outlasts skin in humidity.
4. **Pulse points still work** — wrists, neck — but avoid over-spraying where sweat pools.
5. **Reapply smart.** Carry a small atomizer for late afternoon rather than stacking heavy morning sprays.
6. **Oud in heat:** save dense oud oils for evening, AC spaces, or bakhoor on fabric; see [What is oud?](https://uaeperfume.ae/blogs/guides/what-is-oud).

## Day vs evening

| Setting | Lean toward |
| --- | --- |
| Outdoor midday / commuting | Light citrus, aquatic, soft musk |
| Office AC | Soft florals, clean woods, light oud blends |
| Evening / majlis | Richer oud, amber, bakhoor-layered looks |

## Storage in the UAE

- Keep bottles out of car gloveboxes and direct sun.
- Cool, dark cupboard beats a sunny vanity.
- Cap tightly; heat + open bottles ages juice faster.
